## What it is

Vue DnD Kit is a set of **composables** and one **provider** component. You render your own
elements and hand their template refs to the composables; the library takes care of the rest:
the pointer, touch and keyboard input, finding the target, reordering, animating, auto-scrolling
and telling screen readers what happened.

It renders nothing of its own except a floating **overlay** — by default, a clone of the
element being dragged. Your markup and your styles stay yours.

## What you get

- **Sortable lists and grids** that reorder live while you drag. You render a *draft* of your
  array; on drop, the library writes the new order into your ref.
- **Kanban boards**: lists in the same group exchange items. Copy from palettes, accept plain
  draggables, swap instead of shifting.
- **Trees** with nesting by horizontal offset or by hovering a row, depth limits and rules.
- **Multi-drag** with your own selection (Ctrl-click, rubber band): the block travels together.
- **Animations by default**: items slide to their new places, the drop lands on its slot.
- **Accessibility**: keyboard drags by slots, live announcements, ARIA attributes.
- **Virtual lists**: TanStack Virtual and VueUse, thousands of rows.
- **Controlled mode** for stores and servers: skip the default write and apply the result.

## How it fits together

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `DnDProvider` | Owns the drag: input, the overlay, the selection, auto-scroll, announcements. |
| `makeSortableList` / `makeSortableItem` | A list that reorders, and its rows. |
| `makeSortableTree` | A tree, rendered flat with depths. |
| `makeDraggable` / `makeDroppable` | Free dragging and drop zones. |
| `makeDragArea` | Rules for a part of the page: axis, bounds, snapping, auto-scroll. |
| `makeSelectionArea` | Rubber-band selection. |
| `useDnD` / `useDnDMonitor` | The drag state and its events, anywhere under the provider. |

::tip
Coming from v2? The API changed: `useDnDProvider` became `useDnD`, and `makeConstraintArea` and
`makeAutoScroll` became `makeDragArea`. Sortable lists are now built in.
::

## Requirements

- Vue **3.5** or newer.
- Modern browsers: Chrome and Edge 104+, Safari 15.4+, Firefox 104+.
- Works with SSR and Nuxt: the overlay and the live region mount on the client.
