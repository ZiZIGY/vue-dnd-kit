Vue DnD Kit is drag and drop for Vue 3. Sortable lists, kanban boards, trees, multi-drag, copying
and drop zones — animated, keyboard-ready and announced to screen readers out of the box, in your
own markup.

::example{name="at-a-glance" title="At a glance" hint="Move stops between days; the chips below name the other gestures" height="27rem"}
::

None of it is code of the demo's own: the lists reorder live, the columns trade stops (one `group`),
a selection travels together (`selected`), :kbd{value="Alt"} turns a move into a copy (`clone`),
and the keyboard does all of it by slots. The demo renders the stops and passes a few options.

## What it is

A set of **composables** and one **provider** component. You render your own elements and hand
their template refs to the composables; the library takes care of the rest: pointer, touch and
keyboard input, finding the target, reordering, animating, auto-scrolling and telling screen
readers what happened.

```vue [Day.vue]
<script setup lang="ts">
const listRef = useTemplateRef<HTMLElement>('list');
// `items` is the draft: it reorders while you drag. On drop, `day.stops` gets the new order.
const { items } = makeSortableList(listRef, { items: toRef(props.day, 'stops'), group: 'trip' });
</script>

<template>
  <ul ref="list">
    <StopCard v-for="stop in items" :key="stop.id" :stop="stop" />
  </ul>
</template>
```

It renders nothing of its own except a floating **overlay** — by default, a clone of the element
being dragged. Your markup and your styles stay yours: the library adds ARIA attributes, and a few
`data-dnd-*` attributes to style the states of a drag.

## What you get

- **Sortable lists and grids** that reorder live while you drag, vertical, horizontal or in a grid.
- **Lists that share items**: kanban boards, palettes that hand out copies, swaps, rules for what
  goes where.
- **Trees** with nesting by a sideways drag or by hovering a row, depth limits and rules.
- **Multi-drag** with your own selection (Ctrl/Cmd-click, a rubber band): the block travels
  together.
- **Drop zones** with accept rules, and drops you confirm, refuse or apply yourself.
- **Animations by default**: items slide to their new places, the drop lands on its slot, a cancel
  flies back.
- **Accessibility**: keyboard drags by slots, live announcements, ARIA attributes.
- **Virtual lists** with TanStack Virtual or VueUse: thousands of rows.
- **Sensors and actions**: your own input devices, and drags scripted from code.
- **DevTools**: a tab with a live map of every list, a joystick and a log of each drag.

## How it is different

- **Your data stays yours until the drop.** You render a *draft* of your array. While you drag,
  only the draft moves; on drop the library writes the new order into your ref, or hands you
  `e.result` to apply yourself (stores, servers, undo).
- **No wrapper components.** Rows are your elements, with a ref and one call, so the components
  of a UI kit (Nuxt UI, Vuetify, your own) drag as they are.
- **Animated by default, never in your way.** Reorders, landings and cancels animate out of the
  box; turn them off, tune them, or hand them to GSAP or motion.
- **The keyboard is not an afterthought.** Every drag can be done with :kbd{value="Space"} and the
  arrows, moves are announced, and focus comes back where it was.
- **One provider for the whole app.** Areas give parts of the page their own rules (an axis,
  bounds, a grid, a longer press), so nested features don't need nested providers.
- **Input is pluggable.** Pointer and keyboard are sensors; write your own for a gamepad or a
  voice command, or move items from code with `@vue-dnd-kit/actions`.

## How it fits together

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `DnDProvider` | Owns the drag: input, the overlay, the selection, auto-scroll, announcements. |
| `makeSortableList` / `makeSortableItem` | A list that reorders, and its rows. |
| `makeSortableTree` | A tree, rendered flat with depths. |
| `makeDraggable` / `makeDroppable` | Free dragging and drop zones. |
| `makeDragArea` | Rules for a part of the page: axis, bounds, snapping, auto-scroll, activation. |
| `makeSelectionArea` | Rubber-band selection. |
| `useDnD` / `useDnDMonitor` | The drag state and its events, anywhere under the provider. |

::tip
Coming from v2? The API changed: `useDnDProvider` became `useDnD`, and `makeConstraintArea` and
`makeAutoScroll` became `makeDragArea`. Sortable lists are now built in.
::

## Requirements

- Vue **3.5** or newer.
- Modern browsers: Chrome and Edge 104+, Safari 15.4+, Firefox 104+.
- Works with SSR and Nuxt: the overlay and the live region mount on the client.
