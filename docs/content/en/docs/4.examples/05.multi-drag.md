## What to look at

- **`Gallery.vue`**: `makeSelectionArea` on the area around the grid; it fills its box, so a band
  starts anywhere on it. Render the band from `isSelecting` and `style`.
- **`Shot.vue`**: `selected` is bound to the provider's selection; Ctrl/Cmd-click toggles it.
- In a grid, the overlay of a multi-drag is a pile: the grabbed shot on top, a few others behind
  it, the count on it.
