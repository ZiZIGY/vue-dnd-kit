## What to look at

- **`Tiles.vue`**: the options are getters, so they are read live and the switches change the
  list as it is.
- **`Tile.vue`**: with `placeholder: 'indicator'`, `indicator` says where the drop line is next
  to the tile (`before` or `after`); the list itself doesn't move.
- `behavior: 'swap'` exchanges the dragged tile with the one under it, instead of shifting the
  others.
