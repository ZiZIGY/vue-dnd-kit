## What to look at

- **`Playlist.vue`**: `axis: 'y'` and `modifiers: [restrictToParent]` keep the tracks on their
  axis and inside the list; the ends of the list stay in reach.
- **`Track.vue`**: `handle: '.grip'` — only the grip (a `<button>` with an `aria-label`) starts
  a drag. `index` follows the draft, so the numbers change while you drag. The row has a
  background of its own: the overlay is a copy of it, and a copy shows only what the row paints.
- **`AppGrid.vue`**: `strategy: 'grid'` sorts in two dimensions; `overlay: AppOverlay` lifts the
  icon alone instead of a copy of the tile.
- The slot a dragged item will land in is styled with `[data-dnd-placeholder]::after`.
