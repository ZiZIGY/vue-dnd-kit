## What to look at

- **`Column.vue`**: lists with the same `group` exchange items; the drop is written into
  `column.cards` (a `toRef` of the prop). `dropzone: zone` makes the whole column the target, so
  a card let go below the last one lands at the end.
- **`Card.vue`**: a sortable item with a background of its own (`Canvas`), so its overlay looks
  like the card.
