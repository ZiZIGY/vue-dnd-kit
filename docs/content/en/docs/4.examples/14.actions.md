## What to look at

- **`TrackRow.vue`**: `moveItem(id, { index })` for the arrow buttons, and
  `startDrag(id, { type: 'keyboard' })` to hand the row to the arrow keys.
- **`Queue.vue`**: the shuffle awaits one `moveItem` after another; the scripted drag starts
  with `startDrag`, glides with `moveTo` and lands with `drop()`.
