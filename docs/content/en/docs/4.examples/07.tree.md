## What to look at

- **`FileTree.vue`**: `makeSortableTree` with `indentation`, `collapsed` and
  `canNest: (_node, parent) => !!parent.folder` — only folders take children.
- **`TreeRow.vue`**: each row is a sortable item keyed by `node.key`; its left padding follows
  `node.depth`, also while you drag, so the slot shows the depth the drop will take.
