## What to look at

- **`Board.vue`**: the columns are a horizontal sortable list of their own, and each column is
  a sortable item dragged by its header (`handle`).
- **`Column.vue`**: the cards of every column are one `group`. `put` is a function that counts
  the incoming cards, so a column at its WIP limit refuses the drag and the slot stays home.
  An async `onBeforeDrop` asks before a card enters Done, and answers `'accept'`, `'reject'` or
  `'continue'` (keep dragging).
- **`Card.vue`**: `selected` from `makeSortableItem`, bound to Ctrl/Cmd-click: a multi-drag
  moves the whole selection.
- **`Trash.vue`**: a plain drop target that takes cards only (`accept: 'card'`) and removes them
  with `e.source.remove()`.
- **`CardOverlay.vue`**: the cards' overlay component (the list's `overlay`), built from
  `DragPreview` clones: the card tilts while dragging, the rest of a selection piles up under
  it, and it straightens as it lands (`status`).
