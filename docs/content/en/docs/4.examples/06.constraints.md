## What to look at

- **`Board.vue`**: `makeDragArea(board, { axis, restrictToArea, modifiers })` sets the rules for
  every drag that starts on the board. The options are refs and a getter, read when a drag
  starts, so the switches apply to the next drag.
- The board is also a drop target; `onDragEnd` puts the note at `position`, the `left`/`top`
  where it was dropped.
- **`Note.vue`** only says it can be dragged: the area does the rest.
